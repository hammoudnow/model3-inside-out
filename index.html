ABOUT THIS STUDY

A model, not a factory blueprint.

This independent learning experience uses a credited 2024 Tesla Model 3 exterior. The battery, frame, drive unit, energy paths, and exploded offsets are schematic illustrations built for explanation. They do not represent an exact Tesla disassembly, pack architecture, module count, or performance simulation.

3D exterior

“2024 Tesla Model 3” by RBLXSupercars. Download-page uploader: brandonleong28. CC BY 4.0. Retrieved from the credited public copy. Changes: scale normalization, presentation materials, grouping and animated offsets; original textures preserved.

City ride

The city streets, buildings, trees, street furniture, and ride controls are original code-built scenery. Cruise speed and wheel rotation are visual demonstrations, not a vehicle dynamics simulation.
